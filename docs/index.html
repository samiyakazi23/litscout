Literature reviews where every citation is checked.

LitScout is an AI research agent. Give it a question and it plans searches, reads hundreds of papers, follows their citations, and writes a structured review. Every citation is a real paper, and every cited sentence is checked against that paper's abstract before you see it.

  1. Plan sub-topics and search queries
  2. Search OpenAlex and arXiv, then snowball through references and citing papers
  3. Screen every candidate for relevance
  4. Extract task, method, data and result, with a verbatim quote
  5. Write only from numbered evidence cards
  6. Verify each sentence against the cited abstracts; cut what fails

    Does it find what experts cite?

    Each published survey paper's reference list is a list of papers experts judged important. LitScout gets only the survey's topic and the year it was written, and we measure what share of that reference list it finds, against a single keyword search ranked the same way.
